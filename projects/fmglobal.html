MODERNISE THE PLANE WHILE IT'S FLYING

Insurance workloads don't get maintenance windows. The mandate: move FMGlobal's critical insurance workloads onto secure Azure AI infrastructure — and modernise the GenAI platform around them — without a minute of downtime.
